Our Story

How We Met

Peter and Mary met at Veracity Athletics, where Peter is a coach and Mary is a member. At the gym's Homecoming Cup, Mary was volunteering and Peter was working. With what they now know was some intentional planning by their friend and Head Coach, Amanda, the two ended up paired together for most of the day. As they talked, they quickly discovered how much they had in common, including the surprising realization that their families already knew each other from St. Vincent de Paul and CYO basketball. (Go Knights!)

Their friends at the gym noticed a spark almost immediately and began doing a little matchmaking behind the scenes over the next few months. As Peter and Mary became friends, the other coaches noticed that Mary seemed to find plenty of reasons to stick around after class and talk to Peter. Peter, however, took a bit longer to catch on.

Eventually, after a particularly exhausting workout, Peter and Mary found themselves outside trying to cool off, and Peter finally worked up the courage to ask her out. Their first date was a short walk from the gym, and soon, dates around Summit became a regular thing. They bonded over good food, working out, their Catholic faith, and a shared love for their families. Some of their early dates included going to Mass and admiring the stained glass and architecture of different churches, while others were simply about finding an adventure and somewhere good to eat.

As their relationship grew, Peter and Mary found joy in the everyday things they could share, whether it was tackling a CrossFit WOD, cooking a meal together, heading out for trivia night, or settling in for a board game. Even more importantly, they each quickly came to love the other's family. Time together meant Chicken Cutlet Mondays, fiercely competitive battles for the title of King of Tokyo, and sharing traditions like pizzelle making and the Feast of the Seven Fishes. Their families had been part of the connection from the very first day, and before long, each other's traditions began to feel like their own.

Ocean City has always held a special place in Mary's heart and has been the setting for countless de Poortere family memories. On Peter's first trip there with the family, he and Mary took a long walk together to the end of the island. When they returned to Ocean City this year, Peter suggested they take that same walk again. Mary thought they were simply revisiting a special memory, but Peter had something more in mind. During their walk, Peter asked Mary to marry him, turning a place already filled with so many special memories into one they will now share forever.

Looking back, Peter and Mary can see God's hand in their story every step of the way, from the people who helped bring them together to the faith, families, and experiences that brought them closer. They are incredibly grateful for the path that led them to each other and can't wait to begin their marriage surrounded by the family and friends who have been part of their story along the way.
