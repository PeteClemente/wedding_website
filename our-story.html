Our Story

How We Met

Peter and Mary met at Veracity Athletics, where Peter was a coach, and despite all evidence apparently pointing in the right direction, it took him a little while to realize that Mary liked him. Eventually, after a particularly tiring workout, the two found themselves outside cooling off, and Peter finally worked up the courage to ask her out.

Their first date was at the Greek Grill in Summit, and before long, dates around Summit became a regular thing. They quickly bonded over a shared love of good food, working out, their Catholic faith, and spending time with family. Mass became part of some of their earliest dates, while plenty of others revolved around finding something good to eat. Over time, board games, video games, and a healthy dose of competition found their way into the mix too.

A year and a half later, Peter joined the De Poortere trip to Ocean City, a place that has always been special to Mary and her family. The year before, they had taken a long walk together along the beach, so when they returned, Peter suggested they take that same walk again. This time, he had a surprise waiting. Somewhere along the way, he asked Mary to marry him, and thankfully, it didn't take her nearly as long to figure out how she felt.

Through it all, their story has been shaped by faith, family, laughter, good food, and simply loving the life they've built together. Now they can't wait to begin the next chapter and to celebrate it surrounded by the family and friends who mean so much to them.
